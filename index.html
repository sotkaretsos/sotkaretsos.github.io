Sotirios T. Karetsos

Assistant Professor, Informatics Laboratory, Department of Agricultural Economics and Rural Development

I am an Assistant Professor at the Informatics Laboratory of the Department of Agricultural Economics and Rural Development, Agricultural University of Athens. I have worked at the Ministry of Rural Development and Food, served as a Seconded National Expert and external collaborator at the Joint Research Centre of the European Commission in Italy. I have published more than 80 peer-reviewed papers and 67 reports for the European Commission's Joint Research Centre, and I have served working groups of several funded projects.

Research themes

My current work falls into four themes. Each links to the articles, posters and software behind it.

Blockchain traceability for agri-food

Records that travel with the product and can be audited — olive oil, fisheries, dairy, honey and pork — and the skills supply-chain actors need to use them.

  • ArticleBlockchain and IoT technologies for food traceability in olive oil supply chains · Sensors, 2024
  • PosterCarbon-footprint monitoring in early-stage olive oil production · HAICTA 2026
  • PosterBlockchain-based traceability in fisheries · 2024
  • PosterBlockchain traceability for Greek honey · 2023
  • PaperEvaluating a blockchain training course for food supply-chain stakeholders · ICERI 2024
  • DemoEVOO Carbon Ledger · Pork Traceability

AI and chatbots for agriculture

How chatbots and large language models can advise farmers and support agricultural education, and how far their answers can be trusted.

  • ArticleChatbots in agriculture: a literature review · IEEE Access, 2026
  • ChapterGaiaMentor AI for farmers: a conversational solution for smarter agriculture · IGI Global, 2025
  • PaperEvaluating large language models for educational use in beekeeping · EDULEARN26
  • PosterWheatBot, a generative-AI assistant for wheat growers
  • PosterAI tools for beekeeping education · 2025

Digital farming tools and mobile apps

Mapping and building the apps that support growers through the crop cycle, and what makes farmers adopt them.

  • ArticleA mobile app for supporting citrus fruit growers in Greece · Electronics, 2022
  • ArticleIntention to use drones in agriculture: evidence from Greece · Modern Concepts and Developments in Agronomy, 2022
  • ArticleInvestigating educational mobile apps for agriculture · Int. J. of Sustainable Agricultural Management and Informatics, 2020
  • PosterDigital farming tools in strawberry cultivation and the StrawBot assistant

Digital marketing and e-government in agri-food

How agri-food businesses and public services use the web, social media and mobile channels to reach consumers, farmers and citizens.

  • ArticleSocial media analytics for evaluating digital marketing performance: olive oil enterprises in Greece · Information, 2026
  • ArticleExploring augmented reality marketing in the food and beverage industry · WSEAS Trans. on Business and Economics, 2026
  • ArticleEvaluating social media marketing in the Greek winery industry · Sustainability, 2023
  • ArticleMobile government: a challenge for agriculture · Government Information Quarterly, 2008
