About

I am an Assistant Professor at the Informatics Laboratory of the Department of Agricultural Economics and Rural Development, Agricultural University of Athens. I hold a BSc and an integrated MSc in Agricultural Biotechnology from the Agricultural University of Athens, an MBA in Economic–Engineering Systems from the joint postgraduate programme of the National Technical University of Athens and the University of Piraeus, and a PhD from the Informatics Laboratory of the Agricultural University of Athens.

I have worked at the Ministry of Rural Development and Food, served as a Seconded National Expert and external collaborator at the Joint Research Centre of the European Commission in Italy, and worked as a Researcher at the Agricultural University of Athens. I have published more than 60 papers in scientific journals, edited volumes and refereed conferences, and more than 60 reports for the European Commission, and I have served on working groups of several funded projects.
